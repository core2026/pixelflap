HOW TO PLAY

  • Space / Arrow Up / Tap Screen: Jump / Thrust upward.
  • Pipes: Avoid colliding with top & bottom obstacles. Gap gets tighter as score increases!
  • ⭐ Stars: Collect for +5 Bonus Points (+10 with 2x score active).
  • 🛡️ Shield: Absorbs one collision with a pipe, comet, or boundary.
  • ⏱️ Slow-Mo: Temporarily slows down overall speed for 5 seconds.
  • ⚡ 2X Score: Doubles points for pipes and stars for 6 seconds.
  • ☄️ Comets: Red moving hazards starting at score 12!
